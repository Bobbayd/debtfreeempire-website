Why people look for an EveryDollar alternative

EveryDollar's free plan works for zero-based budgeting, but manual entry is awkward — the app is clearly built around its paid bank-sync tier. The Premium plan runs about $79.99 a year, and without it you lose features that feel essential once you've seen them.

Some users also find the Dave Ramsey debt-snowball framework limiting. If your situation calls for a different payoff order — targeting high-interest debt first, for example — EveryDollar's structure pushes back against that.

What does Paper & Prosperity do differently?

Paper & Prosperity is built for manual entry from the start. The layout is calm and fast — add your income, list your bills and debts, and your budget is ready in a few minutes. No upsell to connect a bank account.

The debt payoff tracker lets you choose your own payoff order. Snowball (lowest balance first), avalanche (highest interest first), or whatever makes sense for your situation. A simulator shows your debt-free date and total interest for each approach so you can compare before you commit.

When is EveryDollar still the better choice?

EveryDollar's Premium plan with bank sync is a good fit if you want transactions pulled in automatically and you follow the Baby Steps framework. Paper & Prosperity doesn't import transactions — every entry is manual. If automation is important to you, EveryDollar Premium or a bank-connected app will suit you better.

Common questions

Does Paper & Prosperity use the debt snowball?
The debt simulator supports any payoff order. You set the priority — smallest balance, highest interest, or a custom order — and it recalculates your debt-free date.
Is there a free trial?
Paper & Prosperity is free with no trial period or credit card needed. Open the web app and start budgeting immediately.
Where is my data stored?
On your own device, with optional iCloud sync on iOS. Nothing is stored on Debt Free Empire's servers, and there is no account to create.

Why choose a calmer budget app

Free, without a paywall to the good parts

Paper & Prosperity is completely free: no ads and no subscription. Features like the Debt Simulator and Savings Vaults are included.

Private by design

Your personal financial data stays on your own devices. If you choose to sync, it goes through your own iCloud account. If you use the optional Your Money Coach, a summary of your budget numbers is sent to an AI service to answer your question.

Zero-based budgeting without the pressure

Like EveryDollar, Paper & Prosperity is built on the idea that every dollar has a job. The difference is the tone: gentle, clear and judgment-free.

When EveryDollar may be the better fit

If you like the Ramsey-style baby steps, want built-in coaching content, or want bank-connected transactions through a paid plan, EveryDollar may be right for you. If you want a free budget app that stays calm and keeps your data with you, try Paper & Prosperity.

Is a free budget app enough to start?

For most people starting out, yes. What matters early on is building the habit of giving every dollar a job and reviewing it weekly. A free app that stores your data on your device lets you build that habit without paying a subscription or connecting a bank account.

If you later want automatic bank syncing or shared budgets for a household, compare options again. You can also see how a different approach stacks up in our YNAB comparison.
