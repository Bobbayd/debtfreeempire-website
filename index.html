Common questions

Isn't entering every transaction manually annoying?

It can be — if you're logging every coffee. Paper & Prosperity is built around scheduled and recurring entries, so your rent, bills, and regular expenses fill in automatically. Most people spend about five minutes a week, not five minutes a day.

I always quit budgeting apps after a few weeks. Will this be different?

Most apps punish you for missing days. Paper & Prosperity has rest days — they pause your streak without breaking it, so a busy week doesn't erase your progress. A short weekly check-in (even just 5 minutes on Sunday) is all it takes to stay on track.

Do the apps connect to my bank?

No — and that's intentional. You enter your own numbers. Your financial data never leaves your device. Not for you if you want automatic bank syncing — YNAB or Monarch Money may be a better fit for that.

Is there really no subscription?

Paper & Prosperity is free forever — no subscription, no ads, no hidden fees. Empire Life Dashboard is a one-time $37 purchase with no recurring fees.
