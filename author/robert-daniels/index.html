Author

Robert Daniels

Robert Daniels is the founder of Debt Free Empire, an independent personal finance app company in Southern California. He builds calm, private, one-time-purchase tools for budgeting and debt payoff, made for people who feel overwhelmed by money.

Experience

  • Founded Debt Free Empire and launched its first apps in 2026.
  • Created Paper & Prosperity, a free budget, debt payoff and savings app for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and the web.
  • Created Empire Life Dashboard, a one-time-purchase life planner for Mac and Windows.
  • Publishes budget spreadsheets and printables in the Paper & Prosperity shop on Etsy.
  • Writes the guides and builds the free tools on this site.
