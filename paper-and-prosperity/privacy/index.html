Privacy Policy

Your budget lives on your device. Something leaves it only when you choose to: asking the Money Coach, or sharing an encrypted household budget with your partner.

Read the Policy Get Support

Effective date: October 16, 2026

The Details

How we handle your information

The short version

Paper & Prosperity stores your budget, debts, savings, bills and expenses on your own device. There is no account, no bank connection, no ads and no tracking inside the app. The one exception is the optional Money Coach, explained below.

Stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved in a database on your phone, tablet or computer. We do not receive a copy. If you turn on iCloud sync, your data moves between your own devices through your own Apple account, under Apple's privacy policy. Debt Free Empire never receives or stores it.

Budget Together (optional)

If you choose to share a household budget with a partner, the changes to that shared budget pass through our server so they can reach your partner's device. Each change is encrypted on your device first, with a key made from the invite code that only you and your partner have. Our server stores only these encrypted changes, so neither we nor anyone else can read them. Your personal budget is never shared. Either partner can delete the shared copy from our server at any time (Settings, Budget Together, Leave).

No account, no bank connection

You never sign up, sign in or give us an email address to use the app. The app never connects to your bank, so we never see your bank credentials or transactions.

The Money Coach (optional)

The Money Coach is an AI assistant. It works only when you type a question and send it. When you do, the app sends the following to our server, which passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, so that an answer can be written:

  • the question you typed;
  • a summary of your numbers: income, budget, spending so far this month and last month, top spending categories, number of debts, total debt, the name and balance of your largest debt exactly as you entered it, total savings, and how far through the month you are.

We do not save your question or your numbers on our server. We only keep a short-lived count of how many requests come from a network address, so we can limit usage and prevent abuse. Anthropic handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not type anything into the coach that you do not want sent. If you never open the Money Coach, none of this happens, and the rest of the app works fully without it.

No tracking or analytics in the app

The app contains no analytics, advertising, trackers or usage monitoring. If you tap a link, it opens in your own browser.

No selling or sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. The only third party that ever sees anything is the AI provider above, and only when you use the Money Coach.

Backups and exports

When you export a PDF or CSV, or make a backup, the app saves a file where you choose, or opens your device's share sheet. That file belongs to you and is never uploaded by the app.

Deleting your information

You can delete any entry in the app at any time. Removing the app deletes the data stored on that device. Data you put in iCloud is managed in your Apple account settings.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

If you message us

If you write to us for help, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We never sell or share it.

Visiting this website

The Debt Free Empire website uses Vercel Web Analytics and HeyCatch analytics to count page views and clicks. Both tools may store a random identifier in your browser. Neither tool collects your name or email, and both apply to the website only, not to the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy?

Send us a message through our contact form. We reply within 48 hours.
