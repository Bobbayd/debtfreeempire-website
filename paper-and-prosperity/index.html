About the app

What is Paper & Prosperity?

Paper & Prosperity is a Debt Free Empire brand offering a personal-finance app, spreadsheets, and printables for organizing money and everyday life.

Is the app free?

Yes. The Paper & Prosperity app is completely free.

Where can I download the app?

Visit our app page for current download links and supported platforms.

Do I need financial experience?

You do not need to be a financial expert to begin. Start with the information you know and follow the instructions for the tool you choose.

Does the app sync between my devices?

Yes. The app syncs with your own devices through iCloud.

Where is my financial information stored?

Your personal financial data is stored on your own devices and, if you use sync, in your own iCloud account. Only anonymized budget totals are shared when using Your Money Coach.

Figuring out budgeting

I have never made a budget. Where do I start?

Start with what you know: your income, your regular bills, your debts, and any savings. It does not have to be exact. You can fill in more and correct things as you learn.

What is a zero-based budget?

It means giving every dollar of your income a job, such as bills, savings, or debt payments, so that your income minus your planned spending equals zero. The Monthly Budget in the app is built around this idea.

What is the difference between Snowball and Avalanche?

Snowball pays off your smallest balance first for quick wins. Avalanche pays off your highest interest rate first, which may reduce the total interest you pay. The app’s Debt Payoff tool supports both, and the Debt Simulator shows how different payments may change your estimated timeline.

What if my numbers are not exact?

Estimates are fine to begin with. Update them whenever you learn more. A rough picture you actually use is more helpful than a perfect one you never finish.

How often should I update my budget?

Many people find a short weekly check-in works well, but choose a rhythm that fits your life. Return whenever your income, bills, or goals change.

The app syncs with your own devices through iCloud. The spreadsheets and printables do not sync with the app or with anything else, so if you use more than one, you update your information in each one yourself.
