No. The spreadsheet and printables do not sync with the app or with anything else. They follow the same debt-payoff process and are designed to be used together, so you enter or update your numbers in each one yourself. (The app itself syncs between your own devices through iCloud.)

No. Either one works on its own. Using both gives you a quick view on your phone and a bigger view for planning.

Which debt payoff method should I choose?

There are two common ways to order your debts. With the snowball method you pay minimums on everything, then put every extra dollar toward the smallest balance. Each paid-off debt is a quick win, which helps many people stay motivated.

With the avalanche method you put extra money toward the debt with the highest interest rate first. It usually costs less in total interest, but the first payoff can take longer.

Neither is wrong. The best method is the one you will actually keep doing. Try both in the free debt payoff calculator to see how your debt-free date changes, then track your chosen plan in the app.
