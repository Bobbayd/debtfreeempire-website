Why people look for a Monarch Money alternative

Monarch Money is a well-designed app, but it costs about $99 a year and is built almost entirely around automatic bank connections. If you want to see your finances without linking accounts — or if you've had bad experiences with Plaid dropping connections — Monarch's model works against you.

Monarch also focuses on net worth tracking and investment accounts. That's useful if you're past the debt payoff stage, but adds complexity if you just want a clean budget and a debt-free date.

What does Paper & Prosperity do differently?

Paper & Prosperity is free and built for manual entry. You type in your numbers, which takes an extra minute but means your budget is stored on your device, with no bank credentials shared, no third-party sync to break.

The focus is narrower and intentional: budget, debt payoff, and savings. No investment accounts, no net worth dashboards, no subscription. If those three things are what you need help with right now, Paper & Prosperity covers them without the noise.

When is Monarch Money still the better choice?

Monarch Money is a strong choice if you want automatic transaction import, investment tracking, and a shared household view. It's a fuller financial dashboard than Paper & Prosperity, which is intentionally focused. If you're past the debt payoff phase and want one app to track everything — including investments — Monarch serves that well.

Common questions

Does Paper & Prosperity track investments?
Not yet — the focus is budgeting, debt payoff, and savings goals. Investment tracking is on the long-term roadmap but not in the current version.
Can couples share a budget?
The web app can be opened in any browser, so sharing a login works for couples who budget together. Dedicated multi-user accounts are not available yet.
How long does setup take?
Most people are set up in under five minutes. Add your monthly income, list your regular bills, enter your debt balances, and your first budget is ready.

Why people look for a Monarch Money alternative

The subscription adds up

When you are paying off debt, every dollar matters. Monarch Money costs around $99 a year — money that could go straight to your debt instead. Paper & Prosperity is completely free, no subscription, no ads.

Not everyone wants bank sync

Monarch Money is built around connecting your bank accounts and auto-importing transactions. If you would rather keep your banking credentials private and enter numbers yourself, Paper & Prosperity was built exactly for that. Your budget is stored on your device.

You want to see your debt-free date

Monarch Money tracks net worth well, but it does not have a debt payoff simulator. Paper & Prosperity shows you your estimated debt-free date and lets you see how paying a little extra each month changes that date — which is often the most motivating number you can look at.

When Monarch Money may be the better fit

We want you to choose the right tool. Monarch Money may suit you better if you want automatic transaction imports, detailed spending reports and charts, or net worth tracking across all your accounts in one place. If you want something free, private and focused on getting out of debt, Paper & Prosperity is built for that.
