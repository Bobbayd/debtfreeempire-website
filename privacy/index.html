Your life stays yours. Empire Life Dashboard does not collect, transmit or sell your data.

Our privacy promise

Five simple commitments, in plain language.

No Data Collected

Empire Life Dashboard collects no personal information. We never see what you enter.

Stored Locally

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you choose to sync using iCloud Drive or another folder sync service, your data travels through that service under their privacy policy. Empire Life Dashboard does not collect, transmit or sell your data to any third party.

No sign-up, no sign-in, and no email address needed to use the app.

The Details

How we handle your information

Information we collect

None. Empire Life Dashboard does not collect, store, or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. We never see what you enter into the app.

Your data is stored locally

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you choose to sync using iCloud Drive or another folder sync service, your data travels through that service under their privacy policy. Empire Life Dashboard does not collect, transmit or sell your data to any third party.

No account required

You never need to create an account, sign in, or give us an email address to use Empire Life Dashboard.

No tracking or analytics

The app contains no analytics, advertising, cookies, trackers, or usage monitoring of any kind. If you tap a website link inside the app, it simply opens in your own web browser.

No third-party sharing

Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to sell, rent, or share with third parties.

If you choose to use iCloud Drive or another folder sync service your data travels through that service under their own privacy policy. Debt Free Empire never receives or stores your data.

Backups

When you choose Download my backup, the app saves a file to the place on your computer that you pick. That file belongs to you and is never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Deleting your information

  • Items you delete go to Recently deleted for 30 days so you can undo mistakes, then they are removed.
  • Start completely fresh (every tab), at the bottom of the Dashboard, erases everything the app has stored. If you plan to remove the app, do this first so none of your information is left behind.

Purchases

If you buy Empire Life Dashboard through a store such as the Mac App Store, that store handles your payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

If you message us

If you write to us for help, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We never sell or share it.

Visiting this website

To see which pages are visited, this website uses Vercel Web Analytics. It counts page views without cookies and does not track you across other sites or collect personal information. This applies to the website only. The app itself still collects nothing.

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy?

Send us a message through our contact form. We reply within 48 hours.
