Why do people leave YNAB?

YNAB's four-rule method is thorough, but it has a learning curve. New users often spend the first two weeks figuring out the system before they budget a single dollar. The subscription cost — around $109/year — also makes it a hard sell when you're already trying to cut expenses.

The bank connection is optional in YNAB, but the app is designed around it. Manual entry is possible but clunky compared to apps built for it from the start.

What does Paper & Prosperity do differently?

Paper & Prosperity skips the methodology entirely. You open the app, enter your income and bills, and your budget is set up in about two minutes. There are no rules to memorize and no categories to assign before you can start.

Your numbers stay on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account to create. If you cancel — there is nothing to cancel, because it is free.

When is YNAB still the better choice?

YNAB works well for people who want automatic bank import and don't mind paying for a structured system. If you have variable income and want a method that forces you to plan every dollar before it arrives, YNAB's approach makes sense. Paper & Prosperity is intentionally simpler — if you want automatic categorization or shared household accounts with real-time sync, YNAB covers that better.

Common questions

Is Paper & Prosperity really free?
Yes — the web app, iOS app and Android app are all free with no subscription and no ads. Empire Life Dashboard is a separate one-time purchase.
Do I need to connect my bank?
No. You enter your numbers manually. That keeps your data off third-party servers and makes the app work even if you use cash or multiple banks.
Can I import my YNAB data?
Not directly — Paper & Prosperity doesn't have an import tool yet. Most people start fresh with their current balances and this month's budget.
What does Paper & Prosperity include?
A zero-based budget, a debt payoff tracker with a debt-free date simulator, savings vaults, a cash flow forecast, and a monthly overview — all in one free app.

Why people look for a YNAB alternative

Cost

A yearly subscription is a real expense when you are trying to pay down debt. Paper & Prosperity is a free budget app, so your money goes to your goals instead.

Privacy and bank connections

Some people simply prefer not to link their bank accounts. Paper & Prosperity never asks for a bank login. You add your income, bills, debts and savings goals yourself, and your personal financial data is stored on your own devices.

A calmer way to budget

Zero-based budgeting works because every dollar gets a job. Paper & Prosperity keeps that idea in its Monthly Budget, then adds a Debt Simulator, Savings Vaults and a Bill Calendar in one gentle dashboard.

When YNAB may be the better fit

We want you to choose the right tool. YNAB may suit you better if you want automatic bank imports, detailed reporting, or you are already comfortable with its method. If you want something free, simple and quiet, Paper & Prosperity is built for that.
